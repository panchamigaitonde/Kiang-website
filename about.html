About Kiang

Our story

Kiang Collective grew out of years we each spent filming, leading expeditions and living in the outdoors. We first worked together in Ladakh, and the Himalaya has stayed at the centre of our work ever since.

We started Kiang in 2024. Our first project was a snow leopard expedition in Ladakh for a small group of wildlife enthusiasts. Since then we've filmed across India and abroad, and led journeys in the Himalaya.

Our work has two sides. On films, we work with broadcasters and production houses: behind the camera, in research, and in line and field production. On expeditions, we take small groups into the same kinds of places, with time to watch, wait and learn from the people who live there.

Panchami Gaitonde

Co-founder · Camera, field producer, expedition leader

Panchami spent years in India's trekking and outdoor-education world, leading journeys built on the idea that the best learning happens in mountains, forests and water.

She has worked as a line producer, field producer and second camera on films for broadcasters including National Geographic and Terra Mater. At Kiang she leads story and research, and designs and leads the expeditions. She is now developing her own documentaries as a director.

Pradeep Hegde

Co-founder · Producer, cinematographer

Pradeep has spent more than a decade filming wildlife in remote and difficult terrain. His work, built on long observational shoots, behaviour sequences and camera traps, has appeared with National Geographic, Discovery, Terra Mater, NHK and Roundglass Sustain.

At Kiang he leads production and camera work.
